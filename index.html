Fill Your Grid

Click the empty triangles one by one — the numbers fill in order: first 1, then 2, 3 … Place them wherever you like — that's your strategy!

How to win: take turns calling a number — it gets cut on every grid. When 2 adjacent numbers in a row plus the 1 number below them are all cut, that's 1 mini-triangle. Complete the target number of mini-triangles = BINGO!
